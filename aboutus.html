About CodeyLabs

100% Salesforce-native solutions built to streamline professional services and enterprise operations

CodeyLabs is an official Salesforce Independent Software Vendor (ISV) Partner dedicated to building 100% Salesforce-native managed packages and accelerators that simplify complex enterprise workflows.

What We Specialize In

Founded on over 18 years of enterprise cloud consulting experience, we engineer zero-dependency, low-code applications tailored to solve critical operational bottlenecks across pre-sales, document automation, service delivery, and user experience:

  • QuoteX (Pre Sales Cloud): Multi-dimensional project scoping, resource estimation, and margin protection built to bridge the gap between pre-sales quoting and post-sales delivery.
  • DRTE (Dynamic Rich Text Editor): Pure 100% Salesforce-native document and PDF generation with built-in e-signatures, dynamic email dispatch, and complete audit trails—requiring zero external servers or Word document dependencies.
  • Build Your Own Branding (BYOB Suite): Native UI customization tools, including dynamic branding sets, field label overrides, and list view row highlights.
  • Feedback 360 (F360) & Service Assurance Hub: Native survey engines with conditional question routing, internal timed assessments, and proactive service delivery tracking.

“Enterprise productivity tools should never compromise data governance. Keeping everything 100% native to Salesforce is non-negotiable.”

Why Choose CodeyLabs

  • 100% Salesforce-Native Architecture: Every solution runs natively on the Salesforce platform with no connected apps, external databases, or third-party integrations. Your sensitive customer and deal data never leaves your Salesforce org.
  • Practical, Adoption-First Design: Built from real-world consulting insights, our tools prioritize intuitive interfaces and self-service configuration so teams can deploy fast.
  • Predictable Per-User Subscription: Accessible, scalable per-user/per-month licensing designed to deliver rapid ROI without hidden infrastructure overhead.

Our Values & Goals

  • Security & Trust First: Total compliance and data safety inside the Salesforce boundary.
  • Pragmatic Simplicity: Replacing clunky workarounds and disconnected spreadsheets with clean, user-friendly solutions.
  • Customer-Driven Innovation: Evolving alongside modern Salesforce capabilities—including native Agentforce and AI-assisted workflows—to help service organizations protect margins and scale smoothly.

Recognition & Milestones

  • Official Salesforce ISV Partner: Recognized partner with security-reviewed, AppExchange-published solutions engineered to strict platform standards.
  • Trusted by Global Services Teams: Adopted by growing Salesforce consulting and professional services organizations to standardize fixed-fee scoping and accelerate deal cycles.
