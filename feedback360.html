FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What types of surveys can I create with this tool?

Our tool supports a wide range of survey types, including customer feedback surveys, internal assessments, employee engagement surveys, and market research. You can customize surveys with various question types such as multiple choice, rating scales, and open-ended questions, and tailor them to suit different objectives and audiences.

2. How does the mock test feature work?

The mock test feature allows you to create and administer practice exams with customizable questions and settings. You can set time limits, define minimum score requirements, and enable or disable editing options for final reviews. It integrates seamlessly with Salesforce, providing detailed performance reports and analytics to track progress and results.

3. Can I customize the appearance of my surveys and tests?

Yes, our tool offers extensive customization options. You can adjust themes, background colors, question layouts, and more to align with your organization’s branding. You can also choose between one-column or two-column layouts to enhance the visual presentation of your surveys and tests.

4. How does the dynamic question routing feature work?

Dynamic question routing allows you to tailor the survey experience based on respondents' answers. Depending on their responses, participants may be directed to different sets of questions or sections, creating a personalized and relevant survey experience that adapts to their input.

5. Is the tool integrated with Salesforce?

Yes, our tool is fully integrated with Salesforce, providing a seamless experience within the Salesforce ecosystem. This integration allows for easy management of survey and test data, real-time performance insights, and the ability to leverage existing Salesforce records and workflows.
