Agentforce for Pre-Sales: Automate Your Pre-Sales Workflow

AI-Powered Pre-Sales Automation for Faster, Smarter Deals

Watch the demo→ Book a Scoping Audit

Pre-sales teams are often slowed down by manual tasks like scoping, pricing, and resource planning, which can lead to inaccuracies and longer sales cycles.

Agentforce for Pre-Sales, integrated with QuoteX, is an AI-powered solution that automates this entire process. Sales Engineers can use a simple, conversational interface to instantly generate quotes, build detailed scopes, create timelines, and produce a complete Statement of Work (SOW).

This streamlines the pre-sales workflow, ensuring pricing accuracy, improving efficiency, and empowering your team to close deals faster with data-driven confidence.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is QuoteX?

QuoteX is an advanced Salesforce-native quoting tool designed for service organizations managing both products and services. It helps pre-sales and sales teams accurately scope projects, estimate costs, build timelines, and generate precise quotes directly within Salesforce.

How does QuoteX streamline the quoting process?

QuoteX streamlines quoting by automating manual tasks and reducing errors through features like Scope Libraries, Resourcing Plan Templates, allowing sales engineers to save valuable time and respond to clients faster.

Can I calculate fees and discounts with QuoteX?

Yes, QuoteX allows you to calculate fees, costs, and discounts based on resources, hours, rates, and other factors, ensuring accurate pricing and consistency across quotes.

Can I use QuoteX without Salesforce Sales Cloud?

While optimized for Salesforce Sales Cloud, QuoteX can also be used as a standalone application with platform licenses, providing flexibility for different organizational setups.
